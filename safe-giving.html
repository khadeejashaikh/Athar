Safe Giving

Scammers copy real charities, especially in Ramadan and after disasters. These habits keep your giving safe.

Seven safe habits

  • Use licensed charities only.

    The u.ae portal lists them.

  • Go to the website yourself.

    Type the address instead of tapping a link.

  • Watch for pressure.

    "Give in the next hour" is a warning sign.

  • Never share your PIN, CVV or one-time code.

    A real charity will not ask.

  • Do not hand money to strangers

    or forward donation links. It is illegal without a licence.

  • Check the account name.

    It should match the charity's name on its official site.

  • Keep a receipt

    and report anything suspicious.
