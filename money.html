Donate Money

In the UAE, only licensed charities and authorised government bodies can collect money. Start from these official links, and type the address yourself instead of following a link in a message.

Official channels

  • UAE Government Portal

    The official u.ae list of licensed charities, zakat channels and fundraising rules.


    Open u.ae
  • Emirates Red Crescent

    Relief, sponsorship for families, orphans and students, and seasonal campaigns.


    Visit rcuae.ae
  • Dubai Cares

    Education programmes for children in developing countries.


    Visit dubaicares.ae

Zakat

Zakat is collected through licensed channels, including the National Zakat Platform and the Zakat Fund. The u.ae page lists the current payment options.

Before you give

  • Check the organisation appears on the u.ae list.
  • Do not give cash to strangers or forward donation links. Unlicensed fundraising is illegal in the UAE.
  • Keep your receipt. See Safe Giving for more tips.

Athar does not collect donations. It only links to official websites. Check each link on the organisation's own site before you give.
