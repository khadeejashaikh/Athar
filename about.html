About Athar

Athar means "a lasting trace". We help people in the UAE find safe, official ways to donate money, donate items or volunteer, with points and levels to keep it fun.

What Athar is

A student project and an information directory. Athar does not collect donations and does not endorse or certify any organisation. Check every official link before you give.

Points, badges and your giving log are self-reported and stay in your own browser.
